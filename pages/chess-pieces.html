CHESS PIECES

Meet the 6 Chess Pieces

Each piece has its own unique movement power. learn how they move, what they do, and how they work together to help you win the game.

King

THE MOST IMPORTANT PIECE

The king cannot be captured. Your main goal is to keep it safe if the king is in check and cannot escape. the game is over.

MOVEMENT

1 square in any direction

Queen

THE MOST POWERFUL PIECE

The queen can move in any direction horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. It's the most versatile piece on the board.

MOVEMENT

Any direction(unlimited squares)

Rook

THE STRONGER DEFENDER

The rook moves in straight line, either horizontally or vertically. it's powerfull on open files and ranks.

MOVEMENT

Horizontal or vertical (unlimited squares)

Bishop

THE LONG RANGE PIECE

The bishop moves diagonally. and it can only stay on the color it stat on (white or black squares).

MOVEMENT

Diagonal (unlimited squares)

Knight

THE UNIQUE JUMPER

The knight moves in an L-shape, 2 squares in one direction, then 1 square perpendicular. it can jump over other pieces.

MOVEMENT

L-shape (2 + 1 squares)

Pawn

THE FOOT SOLDIER

The pawn is the most common piece on the board. it can move forward one square, or two squares on its first move. pawns capture diagonally.

MOVEMENT

1 square (2 on first move) Captures diagonally

Quick Facts

  • ✓ White pieces start first.
  • ✓ Each side has 16 pieces(1 king, 1 queem, 2rooks, 2bishops, 2 knights, 8 pawns).
  • ✓ The pieces work together - strategy is key!

Remember

The king is the most important piece, but every piece has a role to play
