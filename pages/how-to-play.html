HOW TO PLAY

Learn How to Play Chess

Chess is easy to learn, but takes a lifetime to master. This guide will walk you through the essential rules and steps to help you start playing with confidence.

01

Setting Up the Board

Place the board so a white square is on the bottom-right corner. Position the pieces in the correct starting order.

View Setup Guide

02

How Turns Work

Players take turns, with White moving first. Only one piece can be moved per turn.

Learn More

03

Capturing Pieces

You capture an opponent's piece by moving your piece to the same square. The captured piece is removed from the board.

View Examples

04

Check

A king is in check when it is threatened by an opponent's piece. The player must remove the threat or move the king.

See Examples

05

Checkmate

Checkmate happens when the king is in check and there is no legal move to escape. The game is over!

Learn More

06

Draw

A game can end in a draw in several ways: stalemate, threefold repetition, 50-move rule, or insufficient material.

QUICK TIP

Before You Play

Keep these key things in mind to get started:

  • ✓ Learn the movement of each piece.

  • ✓ Remember: the king is the most important piece.

  • ✓ Try simple games and practice regularly.

  • ✓ Don't worry about mistakes - they help you learn!

"A true chess master is someone who has made more mistakes and learned from them, rather than a beginner who is afraid to make a move."
