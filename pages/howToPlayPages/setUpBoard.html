SETTING UP THE BOARD

The Chessboard

The Chessboard is an 8x8 grid with squares, alternating between light and dark color. Each player has a side of the board with a light square in the bottom-right corner.

Placing the Pieces

Each player has 16 pieces, arranged in the same order. Here's how to set them up:

1 Place the rooks on the corners.
2 Next to the rooks, place the knights.
3 Then place the bishops.
4 The queen goes on her matching color (white queen on a light square, black queen on a dark square).
5 The king goes on the remeining square.
6 Place the pawns on the second row.

Remember

White moves first, and the board should always be set up with a light square in the bottom-right corner.
