Build Your Foundation in Chess

Before you can master advanced strategies, it's important to understand the basics. This section will introduce you to the chessboard, pieces, and the main objective of the game.

The Objective

The goal of chess is to checkmate your opponent's king. This means putting the king in a position where it cannot escape capture.

Checkmate = Win

If the king is in check and has no legal moves, the game is over.

Basic Rules

Here are the essential rules you need to know.

1. Turn Based

Players take turns moving one piece at a time.

2. Capturing

You can capture an opponent's piece by moving to its square.

3. Check

The king is in Check when it is under attack by an opponent's piece.

4. Checkmate

The game ends when the king is in check and cannot move.

5. Draw

The game can also end in a draw (stalemate, threefold repetition, etc.)
