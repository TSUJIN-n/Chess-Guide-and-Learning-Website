Check

A check happens when the king is in danger. It means the opponent's piece is threatening to capture your king. You must respond to a check right away, or you could lose the game.

THE BASICS

What is a Check?

A check occurs when the king is under attack by an opponent's piece. The attacking piece can be a queen,rook,bishop,knight, or pawn. When is check, the king cannot move to a square that is also under attack.

Example: Check by a Queen

In this position, the black queen is giving check to the white king. The King is danger and must respond.

How to Respond to a Check

1

Move the king to a safe square(not under attack).

2

Block the attack(if possible.)

3

Capture the attacking piece(if possible.)

Remember

If you are in check, you must respond. You cannot ignore it, otherwise it's checkmate!

Key Takeaways

A check means your king is under attack.

You must respond to a check right away.

You can move the king block the attack, or capture the attacking piece.

ignoring a check can lead to checkmate

" Check is a warning respond wisely, or it could be game over."
